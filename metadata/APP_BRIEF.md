# Ice Fishing FM

## What it is

Ice Fishing FM is a single-player ice-fishing puzzle game. Players use a simulated hydrophone to identify and locate fish by their repeating signals, choose a rig, and manage line tension to land the catch. It is for players who enjoy deduction, pattern matching, and fishing games.

## Launch and onboarding

1. A fresh launch goes straight to “Glass Bay” and “Double Knock.” The watch reads “06:00.” The ice has three tappable holes marked “A,” “B,” and “C,” and the orange probe is accompanied by “Drag hydrophone to a hole.”
2. The “Hydrophone” panel starts open on “Channel 2” with “Shallows” selected. “LEVEL” reads “—” until a hole is selected. “Pin reading” and “Set rig” are disabled. Drag the probe to a hole or tap a letter to start listening and enable both buttons.
3. The top icons have the accessibility labels “Expeditions,” “Journal,” “Field guide,” and “Mute sound.” The optional “Field guide” gives the first expedition’s instructions: “Drag the orange hydrophone to a hole. Tune out the hiss, then pin the same double knock at two holes. Compare their levels before you choose a rig.” “Done” closes the guide.
4. The first catch can be landed at “B” with “Channel 3” for a clear reading, “Shallows,” and “Small jig.” Tap “Set rig,” then “Drop line.” Wait for “Listen for the repeat” to change to “Strike on repeat,” then tap it. Use “Hold to reel” and “Hold to ease” to land “Glass perch.” “Paste into journal” files the catch and unlocks “Third Voice.”

There is no separate welcome screen or mandatory tutorial. The English labels below use the British English wording where the two English versions differ.

## Screens

### “On the ice”

The active field shows the current lake and expedition, the watch time, and an illustrated ice map with lettered holes. “Glass Bay” has “A” through “C,” “Reed Flood” has “A” through “D,” and “Old Pier” has “A” through “E.” Tapping a hole or dragging the hydrophone to it selects that hole and updates the live signal. The hole buttons have accessibility labels such as “Hole A.”

- “Hydrophone” collapses or expands the receiver. The accessibility labels are “Hide receiver” and “Show receiver.” When collapsed, it shows the selected hole’s signal strength if a reading exists.
- The receiver shows “Channel 1” through “Channel 5,” a live signal trace, a “LEVEL” percentage, and a second percentage with the accessibility label “Tuning clarity.” Drag the control labelled “Tuning wheel,” or use the minus and plus buttons labelled “Tune down” and “Tune up,” to change channel. Tune for clarity and compare “LEVEL” across holes and depths.
- “Shallows” and “Deep water” switch the listening depth. “Pin reading” keeps a snapshot of the current hole, time, depth, level, and signal trace. Pins appear on the ice and below the open receiver. “Advance watch” appears in expeditions with moving sources and advances the watch by one minute.
- “Set rig” opens the underwater view at the chosen hole. The title reads, for example, “Rig at hole B.” “Shallows” and “Deep water” set the lure depth. “Small jig,” “Copper spoon,” and “Soft grub” select the lure. “Back to readings” returns to the ice map. “Drop line” lowers the lure. In “Last Broadcast,” the action reads “Lower grapnel.”
- “Bite on the line” displays a live trace of the repeating signal. The disabled “Listen for the repeat” becomes “Strike on repeat” after one full loop and a short delay. Tapping it hooks the source.
- During the fight, “Line tension” and the percentage labelled “Line wound” show load and reeling progress. “Hold to reel” winds the line, and “Hold to ease” relieves tension. A quick tap also gives a short burst of either action. The status reads “Wind towards the hole” (“Wind toward the hole” in US English), “Ease the line,” or “Landing the catch.” Landing completes automatically once enough line is wound.
- The active field’s icons open “Expeditions,” “Journal,” and “Field guide.” The sound icon switches between the accessibility labels “Mute sound” and “Enable sound.” After landing, the header reads “Ice Fishing FM” and the bottom tabs are “Expeditions,” “On the ice,” and “Journal.”

### Catch result

The result keeps the current lake and expedition title and shows an illustration, the fish name or “Training beacon recovered,” and a short field note. “Paste into journal” files a fish catch, records campaign completion, adds any earned beacon phrase, and unlocks the next expedition where applicable. Filing the recovered beacon completes the campaign and opens “Free survey.”

After the first catch is filed, “Play the three notes” previews the signal associated with “Third Voice” and becomes “Stop recording” during playback. On filed campaign results, “Open journal” opens “Field journal” and “Expeditions” opens the campaign list. Filing a “Free survey” catch immediately returns to the ice for another round.

### “Expeditions”

The screen is headed “Expeditions.” Illustrated lake sections group the campaign in this order:

| Lake | Expedition buttons |
| --- | --- |
| “Glass Bay” | “Double Knock,” “Third Voice,” “The Ledge,” “Copper Note” |
| “Reed Flood” | “On the Pass,” “Hollow Echo,” “Two Shoals” (“Two Schools” in US English), “Second Phrase” |
| “Old Pier” | “Deep Water,” “Crossed Traces,” “Quiet Circle,” “Last Broadcast” |

An available expedition opens “On the ice.” The current unfinished expedition has a “Resume” prefix, as in “Resume Double Knock,” and returns to its saved phase. Locks mark unavailable expeditions and checkmarks mark completed ones. After filing “Last Broadcast,” each lake gets a “Free survey” button that opens a repeatable session on that lake.

### “Journal”

The tab is “Journal” and the screen title is “Field journal.” Before any catch is filed, it says “Land a catch to add its silhouette and recording.”

- Filed fish are grouped by species. Each card shows a silhouette, species name, rhythm description, waveform, and the lake, hole, and field note for each catch. The location is shown in a format such as “Glass Bay · Hole B.” “Play recording” loops the species signal and displays its live waveform. “Stop recording” ends playback.
- “Training beacon” appears after the first phrase is earned. Its instruction is “Match the end of each phrase to the start of the next. Begin with the count-in.” “Copper Note” earns “Count-in,” “Second Phrase” earns “Crossing,” and “Quiet Circle” earns “Sign-off.” The phrase cards read “Count-in · ● ● ●   →   ▲ ▲,” “Crossing · ▲ ▲   →   ◆ ●,” and “Sign-off · ◆ ●   →   ■.”
- “Play phrase” previews an earned phrase and becomes “Stop recording.” “Place Count-in,” “Place Crossing,” and “Place Sign-off” append those phrases to the displayed order. “Clear order” resets the placement so the phrases can be rearranged.

The “Field guide” is a scrollable sheet opened from the active field. It shows the current expedition title and briefing, then eight fish entries with a rhythm description, depth, lure, waveform, loop length, tap times, and pauses. The timing lines use formats such as “Loop 1.50 s · taps 0.10 / 0.31 s” and “Pauses 0.21 s.” “Done” closes the sheet and returns to the current phase.

## Features

- Twelve sequential “Expeditions” across three lakes, with stationary sources, moving routes, false echoes, and overlapping rhythms.
- A five-channel “Hydrophone” with two listening depths, repeating audio, waveform and level displays, tuning clarity, and up to three readings to compare.
- An expedition watch and timed route puzzles. A lure takes one watch stop to reach a moving source.
- A “Field guide” with expedition clues and eight fish profiles, including visual timing references for playing with sound muted.
- Three lures, animated underwater casts and bites, and a line-tension mechanic controlled with “Hold to reel” and “Hold to ease.”
- A “Field journal” with catch silhouettes, locations, field notes, and replayable recordings.
- A three-phrase “Training beacon” puzzle and a final recovery expedition using “Lower grapnel.”
- Repeatable “Free survey” sessions in all three lakes after the campaign, with a changing source and starting hole.
- A persistent sound toggle and automatic local progress saving. Receiver drawer animations and hole-selection animations respect Reduce Motion.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- “LEVEL” shows “—,” and “Pin reading” and “Set rig” are disabled before a hole is selected. Tap a hole letter or drag the hydrophone to it. In “Double Knock,” comparing two pinned readings is guidance. A selected hole is enough to enter the rig.
- “Pin reading” keeps the three newest pins. The fourth replaces the oldest. A hole may show only its newest pin marker even if several readings from that hole are still in the receiver. Tuning stops at “Channel 1” and “Channel 5.”
- Locked expeditions open in order after “Paste into journal.” Landing alone leaves the result waiting to be filed. Completed campaign expeditions remain visible with checkmarks and cannot be reopened.
- During “Lowering the line,” the depth and lure choices, “Back to readings,” and the cast button are temporarily disabled. Wait for the cast to finish. A failed cast stays in the rig. “Weak echo. The source passed another hole.” means to return to readings and choose a different hole or predict the next stop. “Pass below the hook. Try deep water.” means select “Deep water.” “Pass above the hook. Try the shallows.” means select “Shallows.” “Interest without a bite. Check the lure in the field guide.” means change to the lure listed for the signal’s fish.
- “On the Pass,” “Second Phrase,” and “Quiet Circle” can show “Pin strong readings at two different stops before predicting the next.” Pin clear readings at the strongest hole at two different watch times and two different holes. Then cast at the hole the source will reach on the next watch stop. A failed cast or escape can move the source on, so check the watch and route again.
- “Listen for the repeat” is intentionally disabled until the signal has repeated, including with sound muted. Use the waveform and wait for “Strike on repeat.” If “The line pulled tight. The source slipped free.” appears, the game returns to the rig. Cast again, reel in shorter stretches, and use “Hold to ease” when tension rises. Both line controls disable during “Landing the catch.”
- “Land a catch to add its silhouette and recording.” remains until “Paste into journal” is used. The journal groups catches by species, so repeat catches add entries inside an existing fish card.
- “Place Count-in,” “Place Crossing,” and “Place Sign-off” require all three phrases, and each can be placed once per attempt. If “The joins do not match. Clear the order and compare the phrase ends.” appears, tap “Clear order” and place “Count-in,” “Crossing,” then “Sign-off.” The success message is “Signal joined. Tune channel 4, listen deep, then lift at the strongest hole.” “Clear order” can also undo a correct sequence.
- In “Last Broadcast,” “Join the three phrases in your journal first.” sends the player back to the journal puzzle. “Pin the beacon at two holes on channel 4 in deep water.” means to use “Back to readings,” select “Channel 4” and “Deep water,” and pin at two distinct holes including the strongest one, “D.” Set the rig at “D” in “Deep water” and use “Lower grapnel.” Lure choice is irrelevant to the beacon.
- “Free survey” appears only after “Paste into journal” on “Training beacon recovered.” Each filed survey catch adds a journal entry and starts a fresh round on the same lake. Hole selection, watch, rig choices, and pins reset for the new round. Opening a different expedition or survey replaces the active trip, so only one unfinished trip can be resumed at a time.
- If system audio is interrupted, the screen can show “Audio interrupted” and “The expedition will resume when audio returns.” There is no button on the overlay. Play resumes when the interruption ends.

## Starter content and resume

The campaign, lake maps, and all eight “Field guide” profiles are built in. “Double Knock” is the only expedition available at first. There are no sample journal catches, pinned readings, or beacon phrases on a fresh install.

The app reopens to the current expedition and restores the saved phase, hole, channel, depth, lure, watch, pinned readings, and fight winding and tension where applicable. An unfiled catch returns to its result for “Paste into journal.” Completed expeditions, journal entries, beacon phrases and their placement order, and the sound preference also persist. Playback and in-flight animations begin again from the saved phase.

## Permissions

None.

## Absent

Login, accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, user-generated uploads or free-text entries, account deletion, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are all absent. Journal entries are generated from play.

## Data and support

Game progress, journal entries, and the sound preference stay on the device. The campaign and audio work offline. There is no on-screen Contact or Support control.

## Scanning and health

None.

## Platform

Supports iPhone and iPad on iOS or iPadOS 17.0 or later. iPhone is portrait-only. iPad supports portrait, upside-down portrait, and both landscape orientations. On wider layouts the ice map and receiver sit side by side.

The app follows the system’s app-language preference and includes British English, US English, Traditional Chinese, and Arabic. British English is the development language. There are no region-specific gameplay restrictions. Arabic uses a right-to-left interface while the ice map keeps its fixed hole layout. For example, the first expedition is “طرقتان” in Arabic and “雙叩” in Traditional Chinese, and the opening prompt is “اسحب الميكروفون المائي إلى حفرة” or “將水下聽音器拖到冰洞,” respectively.

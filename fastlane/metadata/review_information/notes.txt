Ice Fishing FM is an offline, single-player game for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. A fresh save opens at Double Knock in Glass Bay with the receiver expanded and no hole selected. No sign-in is needed.

1. Drag the orange hydrophone to a hole or tap its label. Compare the waveform and strength across holes, change the channel and depth, and use Pin reading to save a snapshot. The question-mark button opens the expedition briefing and fish profiles.
2. Open Set rig, choose a depth and lure, and drop the line. An incorrect hole, depth or lure gives feedback and lets you try again. On a bite, wait for Strike on repeat to become available. Wind the reel and ease off as tension rises. On landing the fish, choose Paste into journal to unlock the next expedition. The first catch result also offers a playable clue for Third Voice.
3. Open Journal to view the catch and replay its synthesised recording. Toggle sound on the ice to check that waveforms and the bite cue remain usable when muted. Relaunch to check that the active trip resumes. Later in the campaign, arrange the three collected beacon phrases in the journal. Last Broadcast requires two deep-water pins at different holes on channel 4, including hole D. Recover and file the beacon to unlock Free surveys.

The hydrophone, radio and clock are simulated. The app requests no runtime permissions and uses no microphone, camera, location, Bluetooth or external accessories. AVFoundation plays synthesised audio. Progress and preferences are saved locally. There are no accounts, networking, cloud sync, analytics, adverts, purchases or multiplayer.
